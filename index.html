Discogs connection

Crate reads release data and market prices straight from Discogs. You need your own personal access token — it's free and takes a minute.

  1. Sign in at discogs.com
  2. Settings → Developers → Generate token
  3. Paste it below

Tempo & key look-up

Optional. Without a key, Find tempo & key on a record still works using AcousticBrainz, which is free and needs nothing — but its data stopped being collected in 2022, so it misses a lot. A free GetSongBPM key fills in more.

Your data

Everything stays on this device. Export regularly.

Export backup saves everything — every record with its tracklists, BPMs, keys, grades and shelf order, plus your Discogs token and sync settings. That's the one to keep. Because it holds your token and Firebase details, treat the file like a password.

The CSV matches Discogs' own export layout, so it goes straight back into your Discogs collection with no fiddling. It carries no artwork or tracklists though — Discogs doesn't put them in the export — so after importing, tap Fill in from Discogs to pull those in.

Sync across your devices

Keeps this crate the same on your phone, your iPad and this computer. Everything still works offline — sync just mirrors it to your own Firebase project when you're online. Use the same config and login as your POS app; Crate keeps its records in their own place, so the two never mix.
